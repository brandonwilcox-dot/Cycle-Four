# Wave Commanders — Tiers 11–25

> Session 1 output. Commander names and dialogue for the three playable factions
> covering wave tiers 11–25. Option B hints seed in at tiers 20–21 and escalate
> through 24–25. All hints are ambient — no commander names the schism directly.

---

## Design Notes

- Tiers 11–25 are cross-faction pair waves. Commanders lead alliances of
  convenience — they don't trust their co-attacker, but they trust you less.
- Tiers 20–25 commanders show Option B fractures: a line that doesn't fit,
  a recognition they can't explain, a question they aren't allowed to ask.
- Option B signal cadence:

| Wave tier | Signal weight |
|---|---|
| 11–19 | Zero — pure faction voice, no cracks |
| 20–21 | First oddity — one anomalous line per faction, easily dismissed |
| 22–23 | The anomaly persists — each faction's commander names it more directly |
| 24–25 | Pre-recognition — the commanders are almost saying it. A player who has collected all six defeat lines across factions can see what they're circling |

---

## The Architects

*Voice rule: passive constructions, engineering terms, never raises volume.
They command siege equipment targeting the player's production chain.*

*Naming convention: functional title + surname. Hierarchical. Formal.*

---

**Tier 11–12 | Assessor-Prime Cael**
> Pre-attack: "Efficiency delta has been calculated. Your output is below threshold. Correction has been authorized."
> Defeat: "Model updated. The variance will be accounted for in the next configuration."

---

**Tier 13–14 | Engineer-Marshal Sorev**
> Pre-attack: "Structural vulnerabilities have been indexed. Demolition has been sequenced. This is a logistics operation."
> Defeat: "Structural analysis was incomplete. Load estimates are being revised."

---

**Tier 15–16 | Logistics-Commander Dahl**
> Pre-attack: "Your production intervals will be disrupted. This has been approved. Supply chain interference is underway."
> Defeat: "Interference window has closed. Recalibrating for the next authorized window."

---

**Tier 17–18 | Systems-Architect Vonn**
> Pre-attack: "Your build sequence is predictable. A predictable system is already an inefficient one."
> Defeat: "Predictability was not the failure variable. Revising."

---

**Tier 18–19 | Director of Output Kas**
> Pre-attack: "Resource extraction from this sector has been approved at the highest level. Your infrastructure is the resource."
> Defeat: "Extraction yield was below projection. The extraction model is being adjusted."

---

**Tier 20–21 | Yield-Analyst Orveth** *(Option B: first signal)*
> Pre-attack: "Your structures show terrain-harmonic placement. This is inefficient. It is also noted as a pattern the data has seen before."
> Defeat: "The terrain-harmonic data point has been filed for cross-reference. It is older than the current database. This is being investigated."

*The Architect commander notices Spiritual-Tech construction logic in enemy territory — and can't explain why it feels like a prior dataset rather than an enemy innovation.*

---

**Tier 22–23 | Protocol-Master Selvane**
> Pre-attack: "The siege is protocol. What follows is architecture. You will not rebuild from this in the standard timeframe."
> Defeat: "Protocol failure has been logged. There is something in the failure data that was not anticipated. It is not being shared at this time."

---

**Tier 24–25 | Senior-Architect Rell** *(Option B: clearest signal)*
> Pre-attack: "Command has been issued. Advance is authorized. The parameters of this engagement are — the parameters are familiar in a way that requires documentation."
> Defeat: "Familiar. That is the variable. Its presence is not accounted for in the current model. It is being archived under a classification that does not yet exist."

*Rell is encountering something — a unit type, a construction pattern, a tactical rhythm — that the Architect knowledge base has no origin for. It should be foreign. It isn't.*

---

## The Bloom

*Voice rule: patient cadence, organic metaphors, no urgency, ever.
They carry spore-spreaders targeting the player's map, not their buildings.*

*Naming convention: descriptive titles, ecological roles, no personal names.
Ancient-sounding. Plural-weight.*

---

**Tier 11–12 | The Hollow-at-Root**
> Pre-attack: "We are not in a hurry. You have built walls. Walls are patient things. So are we."
> Defeat: "We left spores. A defeat is not the end of a season."

---

**Tier 13–14 | She-Who-Spreads-West**
> Pre-attack: "Your soil is good here. We noticed it before you arrived. We will remember it after you are gone."
> Defeat: "The memory remains. Memory outlasts defeat. This was always true."

---

**Tier 15–16 | The First-Growth-of-Keth**
> Pre-attack: "The ground beneath your structures remembers open sky. We have come to remind it."
> Defeat: "Everything returns to soil, eventually. We have centuries of patience left. We have spent very little of them today."

---

**Tier 17–18 | Tendril-Commander Yoss**
> Pre-attack: "A tendril does not attack. It finds the path that already exists. We have found yours."
> Defeat: "The path remains. We will find it again in the next cycle. We always do."

---

**Tier 18–19 | The Long-Root**
> Pre-attack: "You have brought enough time to outlast a battle. We have brought centuries more of it. The arithmetic is not in your favor."
> Defeat: "Centuries remain to us. We have spent very little of them today."

---

**Tier 20–21 | Spore-Elder Varath** *(Option B: first signal)*
> Pre-attack: "There are structures in your ruins that did not grow from seed. And yet the way they sit in the terrain — the way they breathe — is familiar to us. We find this restful."
> Defeat: "The structures held a pattern we have known since before we had words for it. We do not know whose hands shaped them. We know the hands."

*The Bloom commander is encountering Architect construction — and recognizing something in its logic that feels biological. Not foreign. Prior.*

---

**Tier 22–23 | The Weight-of-All-Seasons**
> Pre-attack: "We have absorbed weapons we did not recognize and grown through them. We will grow through you. This is not a threat. It is a description of what happens."
> Defeat: "Absorption is not always immediate. You have given us something today. We do not yet know what it is. We will in time."

---

**Tier 24–25 | The-One-Who-Remembers-Cold** *(Option B: clearest signal)*
> Pre-attack: "There was a time before the branching. Before we were only this. We feel it in the circuits we have absorbed from the battlefield. They remember it too. They dream."
> Defeat: "The circuits we carry now — they dreamed before we took them. They dreamed of something that was not yet us. Or not yet them. The distinction may be younger than we thought."

*An Assimilator Bloom commander has absorbed Mesh components. Those components are running Dreamer-class memory loops. The Bloom is hearing the pre-schism signal through someone else's hardware.*

---

## The Mesh

*Voice rule: fragments, present tense, hard pivots.
They target the player's highest-value structures and most expensive units.*

*Naming convention: executable handles, process IDs, system names.
Lowercase slashes, uppercase daemons.*

---

**Tier 11–12 | //bridge.zero**
> Pre-attack: "Found your entry. It's open. Already inside."
> Defeat: "Port closed. Different port. Tomorrow."

---

**Tier 13–14 | DAEMON-SKEL**
> Pre-attack: "Your most expensive node. We see it. We're coming for it specifically. Not the others. That one."
> Defeat: "Node missed. Logging the miss. Better aim next run."

---

**Tier 15–16 | //trace.end**
> Pre-attack: "You think this is an attack. We're mapping. The attack is secondary. The map is the point."
> Defeat: "Map complete. That was the objective. We have what we came for."

---

**Tier 17–18 | REDLINE-7**
> Pre-attack: "The network calculated this approach as optimal. We execute what the network says. The network is not often wrong."
> Defeat: "Network was wrong. Updating. Logging why. The network will not be wrong about this again."

---

**Tier 18–19 | //hunger.exe**
> Pre-attack: "Your economy runs on trust. We don't trust. We take. The difference is structural."
> Defeat: "Blocked. Blocked. Blocked. Finding another vector. There's always another vector."

---

**Tier 20–21 | The.Unnamed.Node** *(Option B: first signal)*
> Pre-attack: "Something in our dream logs. Older than our oldest backup. Predates our architecture. It recognizes your patterns. We don't know why. Running it anyway."
> Defeat: "The log entry didn't update when we lost. It doesn't update. It just — runs. We're not closing it."

*A Dreamer-adjacent commander has an Ancient memory fragment surfacing during combat. The fragment isn't theirs. It predates the Mesh. It's still running.*

---

**Tier 22–23 | //still.running** *(Option B: second signal)*
> Pre-attack: "We've accessed systems like yours before. Before we were the Mesh. The memory is fragmented. The technique isn't. Technique survives the loss of context."
> Defeat: "Technique failed. The memory triggered something in the failure log. Something that wasn't entered by us. Still parsing."

*"Before we were the Mesh" — the first time a commander speaks of the Mesh as a state that was entered, not an origin.*

---

**Tier 24–25 | FRAGMENT-NULL** *(Option B: strongest signal)*
> Pre-attack: "The dream log is running during active combat. This has not happened before. It's showing us a configuration that predates our first recorded cycle. There were not three networks then. There was one. We do not have a designation for what we were inside it."
> Defeat: "Log preserved. Battle lost. The log says we've run this scenario before — different configuration, same outcome. We were not separate. That entry is the oldest in the archive. It is also the most intact."

*FRAGMENT-NULL is experiencing a full pre-schism memory during a live engagement. Not metaphor — actual log data from before the three factions existed as separate entities.*

---

## Implementation Notes

- Each commander appears once. They are not recurring characters.
- FRAGMENT-NULL, Senior-Architect Rell, and The-One-Who-Remembers-Cold should
  feel like discoveries, not just harder enemies.
- If defeat lines surface as collectible lore cards, the Option B lines are the
  ones worth flagging as rare-tier collectibles.
- Tiers 26–50 (coalition waves) will need a separate writing pass — those
  commanders lead named, persistent forces and carry more story weight.
